| About Mail Pawns

Mail Pawns is a graduation project developed by a team of students from Princess Nourah Bint Abdulrahman University, College of Computer and Information Sciences. The project aims to protect users from malicious emails by using artificial intelligence to analyze the content and links of emails.

We chose this idea due to the growing number of online scams and fake emails that target users on a daily basis. Our goal was to develop a smart and easy-to-use tool, even for non-technical users. Although Mail Pawns supports both Arabic and English, we placed a greater focus on the Arabic language — as most intelligent security tools tend to overlook this area. We wanted to ensure that Arabic-speaking users receive the same level of protection and accuracy in threat detection, making the tool more inclusive and beneficial for a wider range of users. Through this project, we aimed to integrate our skills in artificial intelligence and web development to create an effective solution that serves users in real-world situations.
